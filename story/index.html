Our story

I stayed on tretinoin nine months too long because I convinced myself I was making progress.

*SPOILER ALERT* I was not.

Like many, cystic acne is something I have struggled with for most of my life. I've tried topicals, antibiotics, diet & lifestyle changes, OTC and prescription treatments. Between having ADHD and living a busy life, my skin progress tracking throughout these treatments was largely inconsistent. I began judging my skin based on perceived changes, and minimal evidence.

A friend looked at my "after" photo following 9 months of tretinoin use, and asked if it was the "before." It was an innocent mistake, but it made me question how I was evaluating my progress. After this wakeup call, a second look at the photos revealed that tretinoin may have actually been increasing my skin irritation with no real improvement in acne. Shortly after this revelation, I switched to isotretinoin (a.k.a. Accutane) and began seeing real progress within two weeks.

I'm very happy that my cystic acne is virtually gone now, but I wish I'd switched to isotretinoin earlier and saved myself from some scarring!

Gia is the tool I wish I'd had back then: consistent photos, tracking reminders, unbiased analysis, and a straight answer about whether to continue my treatment plan or start looking at other options.
